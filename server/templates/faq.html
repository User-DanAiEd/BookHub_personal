Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about BookHub

BookHub is a comprehensive book management platform that helps you discover, track, and organize your reading journey. You can search for books, track your reading progress, create summaries, and get personalized recommendations.

Use the search feature on the homepage or navigate to the Search page. You can search by title, author, genre, or keywords. BookHub searches multiple online databases including Google Books and Open Library to provide comprehensive results.

After finding a book, you can add it to your tracking list. The system allows you to mark books as "Want to Read," "Currently Reading," or "Finished." You can also add custom tags and notes to each book.

Custom tags are labels you can create to categorize your books. For example, you might tag books as "fantasy," "self-help," or "classic-literature." These tags help organize your library and improve book recommendations.

Our recommendation system analyzes the books in your library and the custom tags you've created. It then suggests similar books based on genre, themes, and user preferences. The more books you add and tag, the better the recommendations become.
